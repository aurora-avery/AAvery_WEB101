Aurora's WoW Forever Launch LAN Party

All-Night Gaming Grind - November 4, 2026!


About the Event

Celebrate the official launch of World of Warcraft: Forever with your fellow WoW nerds. Bring your own rig (and let me know if you will need to borrow mouse/keyboard) or bring your gaming laptop! Whether you want to focus on leveling while reading quest text, speedrunning to max level, or wanting to enjoy open exploration and profession crafting for nostalgia; you shouldn't do it alone!

What to Expect

Prepare to relive your childhood!

  • IRL Grouping: we are all playing the same game, so before you need help with a quest or killing a boss ask someone here to join you!
  • Food Break: I will have bundle snacks like chips and goldfish readily available, but will stop a few hours in to decide on pizza order for all.
  • Caffeine Breaks: Constantly stocked with sodas and a minifridge in arms length but will take 10 minute break every couple hours for fresh made coffee!

Event Schedule

Day 1: Leveling Push (at your own speed)

The start of the long-rumored WoW 2 known as World of Warcraft Forever! It is the original game with upgraded graphics in HD, a new Skyborne race, a promise that the servers for this game will be running forever!

Day 2: Choosing professions and creating guild (you are not forced to join)

I will have a spare monitor, plenty of extention cable outlets, keyboards and mice. However, let me know in advance what you will be needing to ensure I have enough spare input devices for all those who need it! There will be plenty of spare table space and seating but you are free to bring your own chair if you prefer. Snacks and drinks are on me but feel free to bring something - just bring enough for sharing!

RSVP

Azeroth once again is refreshed with new quests, and graphics. Set in the original timeline without Gul'dan or the destruction of the Lich King. Explore what it was like to have an online RPG where random players help you when your health is low, or your guildmates actually hop in the discord to give raid/dungeon instructions. There is no LFG, group finder, and in order to clear a dungeon you must coordinate with a team and make a guild you actually look forward to speaking to!
